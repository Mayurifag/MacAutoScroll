What to check
  1. Middle-click on a plain link → opens in new tab; auto-scroll does NOT activate.
  2. Middle-click on the nested link (text inside an <a>) → opens in new tab. composedPath finds the ancestor anchor.
  3. Middle-click on the link inside the pink-bordered shadow root → opens in new tab. composedPath traverses the open shadow boundary.
  4. Middle-click on the dark background or pink dashed area (no link) → auto-scroll activates and the page scrolls.
  5. Open Settings, uncheck "Middle-click on link → new tab". Reload. Now middle-click on any link starts auto-scroll instead of opening a new tab.
  6. Right-click during auto-scroll on dark area → scroll stops, no context menu.
