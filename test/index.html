What to check
  1. Middle-click on the gradient. Cursor turns into the autoScroll 4-arrow icon. Initial dot is exactly under the click point.
  2. Drag in 8 directions (up, down, left, right, and the four diagonals). Cursor swaps to the matching arrow icon and the page scrolls in that direction.
  3. Move to the very edge of the screen — speed caps (does not run away). Move just barely past the dead zone — scroll is slow.
  4. Toggle mode: middle-click and release without moving. Scroll stays active. Move mouse to scroll. Click any button to stop.
  5. Drag mode: middle-click, hold, move. Release the button — scroll stops on mouseup.
  6. Press any key during scroll — scroll stops.
  7. Right-click during scroll — context menu does NOT appear; scroll stops.
  8. Switch to another tab — scroll stops on blur.
