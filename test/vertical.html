What to check
  1. Middle-click. Cursor is the verticalScroll two-arrow icon (up/down only).
  2. Drag horizontally — page does NOT scroll horizontally; cursor stays vertical.
  3. Drag up/down past the dead zone — page scrolls.
  4. The cursor switches to top or bottom single-arrow as you move past the dead zone in each axis.
  5. Speed scales with distance. Easing slider in popup affects how quickly speed reacts to direction flips.
